## Findings
- Therizino and Teno are far ahead. Both got the biggest jump from "stats maxed for everyone" (v1048): the Therizino's Building
  Fury star finisher (24 -> 51) and the Teno's claw rows (12 every 0.5 s -> 15 every 0.367 s, +70%).
- The Teno is also the tank (167 effective HP), so it kills in 2.1 s and needs 8.8 s to die.
- The Dilo and Troodon are the slowest killers on paper; what makes them feel easy is range + lock-on (every hit lands from 80-108
  studs), not raw damage. After today's x1.15 / x2.5 they sit at the bottom of the damage table.
- Pachy's sustained damage is the lowest of the melee dinos (3 charges, slow refill), its burst is fine.

## Recommendation (not built)
- Therizino damage x0.7 (burst 123, sustained 39; avg kill 2.1 s).
- Teno damage x0.75 (burst 92, sustained 31; avg kill 2.8 s, still the tank).
- Leave Herrera, Pachy, Raptor, Dilo, Troodon; play a few matches after the change before touching them.

### Findings (no numbers changed)
- Fights are about twice as long: average kill with kits 1.1-2.4 s -> 1.8-5.7 s.
- Top killers stay Therizino (combo) and Dilo (kit); Troodon is still last (support kit, unchanged main attack).
- Pachy's main attack is now the weakest (2 charges, 3 s refill: 8.8 s average kill without parts).
- Herrera dies fastest (40 HP without the 0.7 plating rows: 1.5 s with kits); Teno is the tank (6.3 s).
- The Dilo x1.15 and Troodon x2.5 multipliers were set on maxed numbers; with venom add and the faster dart gone the Dilo is
  mid-table on main attack, still top with its kit.
- Options for Kepu (not built): leave as is and play; Pachy headbutt refill 3 -> 2.2 s or 3 charges; Herrera HP 40 -> 55;
  Therizino finisher 24 -> 20.
