(SkillServer 1.18 SkillAllStatsMax), no mecha parts, main attack only, every hit landing. Target: an enemy practice bot pinned in
front (DummyHealth 5000, its shields cleared each frame), damage logged from its DummyHealth. Burst = damage in the first 3 s with
every charge full; sustained = damage per second over 10 s. Effective HP = MaxHealth / StatDamageTakenMul (maxed defence stats).

## Findings
- Therizino and Teno are far ahead. Both got the biggest jump from "stats maxed for everyone" (v1048): the Therizino's Building
  Fury star finisher (24 -> 51) and the Teno's claw rows (12 every 0.5 s -> 15 every 0.367 s, +70%).
- The Teno is also the tank (167 effective HP), so it kills in 2.1 s and needs 8.8 s to die.
- The Dilo and Troodon are the slowest killers on paper; what makes them feel easy is range + lock-on (every hit lands from 80-108
  studs), not raw damage. After today's x1.15 / x2.5 they sit at the bottom of the damage table.
- Pachy's sustained damage is the lowest of the melee dinos (3 charges, slow refill), its burst is fine.
