The catch

The Aug 23, 2025 "admin war" peaked both games at 20M+ concurrent, promoted on TikTok, X and Discord, then both fell back near 1M. Event loot that outshines normal play makes the regular game feel empty.

What this mockup takes from that

  • Fixed slot, announced ahead. A countdown banner on every server so players know when to log in (the "wake up and play" hook).
  • Random strikes on people who are there. Only active players get struck, so being online is the reward.
  • Cosmetic, not power. Event skins and a rare drop; points and eggs, never stats that beat normal progression.
  • Short. 5 to 10 minutes, then back to the regular game.
