Arena Hardpoint · options v0.1

Two teams fight over one hill at a time on the real island. Standing in it scores for your team; the hill moves every 60 s. Picked so far: Red vs Blue auto-balanced, Hardpoint takes turns with free-for-all. Pick a hill layout, a HUD placement and a hill size; then check the rules table. The map is the real island, sampled from Studio (72 × 72, 30-stud cells).

    Still to settle (my recommendation first)

    • Cloaked dinos on the hill: they count for their team, and the hill shows CONTESTED, so the other team knows someone is there. Or: a cloaked dino doesn’t count.
    • Squads: balancing keeps a squad on the same team and moves solo players first; squads of 4 can make teams uneven by 1–2 for a match.
    • Friendly fire: off for your whole team, using the same check the squads already use in every damage path.
    • Joining mid-match: you go to the smaller team (ties: the losing team).
    • Hill look in the world (ground ring, light column you can see from far): its own 3D mockup once these rules are picked.
