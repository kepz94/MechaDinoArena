Arena squads

A SQUAD button under SKILLS opens the squad list: invite players on the island, leave the squad, and see your squadmates' health. Up to 4 per squad. Squadmates can't hurt each other in any way, respawn near each other, and Med Stations heal them too. The screen behind is a real phone capture (Studio, Herrera); the squad pieces are the mockup. Click around: everything on the screen works.

Rules (your picks)

  • Up to 4 per squad, Arena only. Invite anyone on the island who isn't in the menu or in a full squad.
  • The invite pops up on their screen for 15 s: JOIN or NO. Joining someone already in a squad puts you in that squad.
  • Friendly fire off for everything harmful: no damage, slows, stuns, daze, bleed, pins, latches, traps, mines, caltrops or goo between squadmates. Your smoke doesn't hide you from them.
  • Med Station heals squadmates standing in the circle too (your pick: "heals work for both of us").
  • Respawn near a squadmate: 30–60 studs from a live squadmate, any lobby size.
  • Leaving the island, going to the menu, or leaving the game takes you out of the squad. The squad lasts through deaths.
  • PC key for the button: G (like K for SKILLS).
