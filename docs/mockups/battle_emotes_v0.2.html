  • Short wins in a fight. Taunts land at 1.5 to 3 seconds. Long dances belong in the lobby.
  • Moving or attacking always cancels. You choose to be open to a hit; that risk is part of the fun.
  • The camera stays put. Black Ops 4 had to fix emotes that let you look around walls. Ours already play in third person, so nothing changes.
  • One emote, every character (Fortnite) sells better than per-character emotes. Our rig makes that free.
  • Mute and a cooldown (Clash Royale) stop spam from ruining the match.

How you'd fire one · you picked A

A · Emote wheel

☻

A smiley button in the top right. Hold it, slide to a slot, let go. 8 slots, set in the Lab. PC: hold B. Same as Fortnite and Rivals. Recommended.

B · Quick bar of 4

GG⇧☠♪

4 small buttons always on screen. One tap, no menu. Faster, but takes screen space next to the ability buttons and only fits 4.

C · Auto after a kill

KILL · STOMP

No button. Your equipped emote plays by itself after a kill, if you stand still for half a second. Clean HUD, but players can't taunt when they want to.

Rules I'd use

Move or attack cancels itAny thumbstick, jump, ability or bite stops the emote at once and blends back in 0.15 s.
Taking damage cancels itA hit knocks you out of it, so emoting is a real risk, not a shield.
3 second cooldownOne emote at a time, then wait. Stops spam.
Mute other players' emotesA Settings switch. Their dino still moves; you just don't see the effects.
Camera does not changeNo free-look or zoom-out during an emote (the Black Ops 4 lesson).
Props and wordsProps are blocks welded to the head or hand, like the dino parts. Words are a pop-up billboard over the head. Both scale to the dino.
Built like the roarA new EmotePose module on the same joints, started by an EmoteId + EmoteStartedAt stamp. Every number is an attribute; one switch turns it all off.
