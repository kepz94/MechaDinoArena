Thruster boost lean

The raptor's pose reacts to which way you boost, so the flames point away from where you're going. Pick a direction (W / A / S / D on PC, the stick on a phone), press Boost, and compare the options in both views.

Side view (W / S)
From behind (A / D)

Same in both options: the lean eases in over 0.15 s and back out over 0.25 s, follows the steering during the boost, and plays on every player's screen (worked out from the boost start and the dino's movement). The camera, controls, speed and duration of the boost don't change.
