Credits button · main menu

A CREDITS button at the bottom of the main menu opens a CREDITS panel with every song, its artist and its license. Three places for the button. In every option the button replaces today's small grey credit line, which stays in the code behind its switch. Tap CREDITS in the frame to open the panel.

RECOMMENDED

A · Corner button

A small CREDITS pill in the bottom-right corner, styled like the MUSIC ON pill at the top-right. The two corner buttons mirror each other.

  • Takes today's credit line's spot, so nothing else moves.
  • Easy to find, doesn't compete with SANDBOX / ARENA.

B · Under ARENA

A slim dark block button, the full width of the right column, right under ARENA.

  • Most visible.
  • Sits in the main play column, so it pulls a little attention from the play buttons, and the right column gets taller.

C · Footer strip

A thin dark bar along the whole bottom edge: a short "Music by Kevin MacLeod · APM Music" line on the left, a CREDITS button on the right.

  • The short credit is always on screen.
  • The bar costs ~5% of the screen height, so the left column moves up by that much.

The panel (all options)

  • Centred card over a dark veil; ✕ or a tap outside closes it. Scrolls if the list is taller than the screen.
  • Lists all 7 songs: the 4 new defaults (Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0) and the 3 kept ones (Gymnopedie No. 2, Little Toys, Crazy Daisy).
  • Each song is tagged with where it plays: MENU, ISLAND or LAB, or KEPT for songs that are no longer the default.
  • Music only, since that's the credit we owe. Other credits (e.g. "Made by") can be added later if you want them.
