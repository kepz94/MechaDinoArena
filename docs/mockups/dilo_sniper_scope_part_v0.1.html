0 · WHAT OTHER GAMES DO

OVERWATCH · WIDOWMAKER
  • Scope in takes 0.32 s
  • Shot charges in the scope: 12 → 120 dmg over 0.9 s
  • Moves 65% slower while scoped

Take: damage builds while you hold the scope, so tap-spam is weak

SPLATOON · SPLATTERSCOPE
  • Holding charges; the scope version goes first person + zoom
  • Full charge = more range + damage
  • Scoped version can't hold a charge

Take: the same ink / venom shot, but the scope trades view for reach

FORTNITE · BATTLEFIELD · HALO
  • Scope glint: a flash on the sniper that everyone can see
  • Lets targets spot the sniper and take cover

Take: a red glint on the monocle while scoped, so it's fair

BRAWL STARS · PIPER
  • Mobile sniper: drag to aim, let go to shoot
  • Hits harder the farther the target is

Take: your press · drag · let go fits phones; the range bonus is optional

OUR VERSION

ATTACK · MECHA PART · BUTTON · MONOCLE ARM

SNIPER SCOPE

  1. Button: press to swing the lens over your eye and scope in (0.2 s). The shot charges while you hold: 15 damage at once, up to 40 at full charge (1 s). Let go: one venom shot hits instantly, plus dart venom. Separate from your 5 spits. Recharges in 10 s
  2. Reaches farther (150 → 200 studs)
  3. Recharges in 8 s
  4. A hit marks the dino for your squad for 3 s
