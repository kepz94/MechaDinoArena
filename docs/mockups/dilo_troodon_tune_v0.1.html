Troodon: tap for enemies, double tap for teammates

How it works

Single tap: throws at the closest enemy in front, as now, with no extra delay.

Double tap (second tap within 0.3 s): the same pod, still in your hands (it leaves after 0.34 s), switches to the closest teammate in front instead. One pod, one charge. No teammate in reach: it lands straight ahead.

The aim ring under the target turns green on a teammate.
