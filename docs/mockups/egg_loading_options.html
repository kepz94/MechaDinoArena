Egg hatch loading screen

Three looks for the screen you see while the game loads, on join and on every Sandbox ↔ Arena trip. In all three the egg keeps cracking while the game loads. When it's ready, the shell pops and a baby T-Rex sits up inside. Two seconds later the screen fades into the menu. Use the controls to replay it, try a slow load, or switch to the Arena trip.

Recommended

A · Nest

The egg sits big in the middle of a straw nest under a night sky, with the game title above and a yellow progress bar under it. Easiest to read on a phone, and the bar tells you how long is left.

B · Beach sunrise

The egg sits on the island's sand at sunrise, and the title sits bottom-right. No bar: the cracks and dots are the only progress. The prettiest scene, but you can't tell how long is left.

C · Mecha incubator

The egg sits in a metal incubator pod with a glowing gauge. The lid lifts when it hatches. Ties into the mecha theme, but less "cute nest" than you described.

How it would be built

  • Drawn from plain blocks (UI frames), not images, so it shows on the very first frame, before anything has downloaded. Same block style as the island.
  • Runs from ReplicatedFirst and replaces Roblox's default loading screen. Cracking keeps going until the game reports it has loaded and your dino is parked behind the menu.
  • For a Sandbox ↔ Arena trip, the same screen is shown while you travel and keeps cracking on the other side until that place has loaded. The caption reads "Traveling to Arena" / "Traveling to Sandbox".
  • Baby is always a T-Rex. After it pops out: 2 seconds, then a 0.6 s fade into the menu.
