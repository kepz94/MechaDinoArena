EMOTE WHEEL · WHERE IT LIVES AND HOW YOU PICK YOUR 8

The phone below is the live Phone HUD v2 layout, measured in Studio (750 × 381, iPhone 17 Pro): ☰ top-right, score strip top-centre, health top-left, JUMP / ATTACK and the two part buttons bottom-right. Each option adds the emote wheel to it. Tap the emote button, then tap an emote to try it. You own all 12 emotes; 8 fit on the wheel.

1 · Phone: how you open the wheel

PC: hold B (Fortnite's key). B already opens the spawn picker for a few seconds after you respawn (RespawnScreen), so in that window B stays the picker. X is the other free key. The same 8-slot wheel opens in the middle of the screen, move the mouse toward a slot, let go. Same wheel on every option.

2 · Lab: where you pick your 8

Tap a card, then PUT ON WHEEL, then tap a slot. Locked ones show their challenge; paid ones say 149 R$ and open the Robux prompt. Switch NEW PLAYER to see what a fresh account gets (GG Party only).
