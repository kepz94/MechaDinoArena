HOW IT WORKS (ALL OPTIONS)

Tap the egg → Roblox's own favorite window (the game). Favorite → the roll. Cancel → "FAVORITE TO ROLL", ask again any time. Roblox has no like window and no way to check likes, so only the favorite is checked; the sign still asks for both.

Prize: rarity first (odds, all attributes: Common 60% · Rare 30% · Epic 5% · Legendary 2% · Dino 3%), then any item of that rarity you don't own yet. A dino comes owned with every mecha part maxed. One roll per player, ever. Timer: 48 h from the first time you see the egg; at zero the egg is gone for you. Picked: B · Prize reel.

Place: the plaza rim at 225°, across from the ARENA portal (shop 160°, quests 290°). Sizes here are the phone sizes; every button is at least 44 px.
