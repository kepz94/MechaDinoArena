A · Full disguise RECOMMENDED

On the island and in Arena matches, bots look exactly like players: player-style names, W-L, skins, trails, parts, emotes. There is no BOT tag anywhere except in the VS BOTS match.

B · Island only

Island bots look like players. In Arena matches the filler bots get the same names, skins and parts, but the lineup, scoreboard and kill feed keep the BOT tag, as they do today.

C · Reveal at results

During the match it works like A, with no tags. On the results screen after an Arena match, each bot gets a small AI tag, so players find out afterwards who was real.
