Tap-and-pull mecha buttons

Each of the 2 mecha buttons holds 2 parts: tap = its top part, tap and pull down = its second part. That makes 4 loadout slots. Try it: press a button, drag down past the line and let go for the second part; let go without pulling for the first. The example loadout is the raptor's.
