Rebuilt to the marketing rules. Every dino was filmed in Studio in front of a flat backdrop, cut out, and placed on a designed background with a thick outline. Answer with IDs, for example "Icon V1, thumbnails T1 T2 T3 T4, title 1".

ICON RULES APPLIED

  • One subject filling the frame on a clean high-contrast background, no scenery.
  • Emotion and action: jaws open in a roar, Shock Cannon charged.
  • Shows the fantasy in one look: a dino with a mecha weapon.
  • Heavy white and dark outline so it pops off the Roblox grid.
  • No title text. At 150 px text is noise. V1 + NAME is only here because you asked to see both.
