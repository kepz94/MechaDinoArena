Press: launch, view jumps into the missile high above · steer with the mouse / drag your thumb · BOOST once to dive fast · hits: 40 to every enemy in the ring, 0 to your team

3 · THE PART (LAB CARD)

ATTACK · MECHA PART · BUTTON
CRUISE MISSILE
  1. Button: launch a missile from your back and fly it down onto the map yourself. 40 damage to every enemy in a 45-stud blast (the size of the point), 0 to your team. You stand still while you fly it. Recharges in 45 s
  2. Bigger blast (45 → 55 studs)
  3. Recharges in 35 s
  4. Leaves a burning crater for 4 s (8 damage a second to enemies in it)

Flight: up to 8 s from 250 studs up; steer speed slow, BOOST once (3x dive). If you're killed while flying, the missile drops straight down where it is.
