The rules shown here

Auto-climbRun into any palm, jungle or pine trunk (trees only) and it grabs on and climbs straight up while you hold forward; let go and it clings. At the top it pulls itself onto the tree top. Space hops off backward; walking backward slides down.
Tree topsJungle canopy blocks, pine leaf tiers and a flat pad over each palm's fronds become solid for the Herrera only. Other dinos still pass through leaves like now.
Dash-through slash (LMB)About 25 studs in 0.3 s; claws every dino it passes through once: 8 damage + bleed; ends behind them with a half-second speed burst. 2 charges, one refills every 4 s. No dash while stunned, pinned or in the menu; a miss still uses the charge.
Drop attackThe same dash, used on a trunk or a tree top: aimed at a dino within about 40 studs below and in front, it dives at them and slashes on the way down. No dino in range: it leaps off forward.
Bleed3 damage per second for 4 s; a new slash restarts the timer (no stacking). The Energy Shield blocks it.
Body40 HP (the lowest), fastest run (about 15% over the raptor).
