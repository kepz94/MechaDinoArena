What's different now

  • Posture: long and low, body held level, neck pushed forward, tail dead straight. The raptor stands upright with a raised tail.
  • Wedge head: tall boxy back of the skull tapering to a long snout.
  • Snout window: a dark opening on each side of the snout.
  • Hooked snout tip with a notch and a big fang behind it.
  • Red horns over the eyes and a red throat pouch.
  • Deep, kinked lower jaw full of teeth.
  • Shoulder hump with a row of dark back scutes down the neck, back and tail.
  • Slanted tiger stripes on the flanks and thighs (the raptor has straight bands).
  • Big three-claw hands held forward; long legs; no sickle claw.
