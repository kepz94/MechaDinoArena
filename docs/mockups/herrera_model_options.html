What's new in v0.4

  • Neck frill: an 11-plate fan behind the head with dark spokes and a red rim. Fully open from the front; the raptor has none.
  • Hunched goblin back: barrel chest tipped forward, a shoulder hump, and 7 black quills rising to the shoulders.
  • Neck hangs down from the hump with a red throat sac; head carried low and forward.
  • Big skull: oversized cranium, bulging eye bumps with big eyes, small back-swept red horns, dark cheek mask.
  • Underbite: lower jaw sticks out past the snout with snaggle teeth pointing up.
  • Long dangling arms that hang to the ground, each with three hooked claws.
  • Skinny stilt legs with knee knobs and three splayed toes.
  • Whip tail that curls up at the end into a quill tuft.
  • Slanted tiger stripes and red spots on the flanks.
