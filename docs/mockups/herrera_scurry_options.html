How it works in game

  • Standing still: the two-legged pose you picked (the model's rest pose), with the normal idle breathing and head turns.
  • Any movement: it drops onto all fours and scurries. The drop and the stand-up blend in about a fifth of a second.
  • Arms: the long dangling arms already reach the ground once the body tips forward; no model change needed.
  • Hitboxes and the camera stay on the body; only the pose changes.
