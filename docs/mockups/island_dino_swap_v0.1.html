CHANGE DINO ON THE ISLAND

Today the island HUD has SKILLS, MENU, SQUAD and the camera button. Changing dino means MENU → the home screen → ◀ ▶ → PLAY, which also drops you back at the portal spawn. Each option below swaps you where you stand, without the home screen. The dinos are the game's own block models. Click the glowing entry in each screen to try it.

1 · ANYWHERE, NOT RIGHT AFTER A HITRECOMMENDED

Works anywhere on the island, but greys out for 5 s after you take damage. A swap gives a fresh body at full health, so this stops "swap mid-fight to heal".

2 · SAFE AREA ONLY

Only inside the portal islet's safe area. Elsewhere the button shows "GO TO THE SAFE AREA".

3 · ANYWHERE, ANY TIME

No limits.

ALL OPTIONS
  • You stay where you are; the new dino appears in your spot with a puff (same swap the home screen's ◀ ▶ does today).
