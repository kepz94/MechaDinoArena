Dino Lab

The Lab replaces the skill tree and the menu's tap-to-pick loadout. Your dino stands in the middle with every upgrade it has, each mech part is a card with the part's own 3D model, and you buy steps, upgrade stats and fill the four button slots in one place. The main menu keeps the dino select with a read-only slot row and a GO TO THE LAB button; the SKILLS button on the island opens a compact version over the game. Everything is clickable: buy steps, equip parts, switch dinos.

Recommended

A · Workbench

Three columns: part cards on the left (PARTS / STATS tabs), the dino big in the middle with the four slots under it, and the selected card's steps with BUY and EQUIP on the right. Everything visible at once; on a phone the cards and details get narrow.

B · Split

Dino and slots on the left half, a 4-wide card grid on the right, and the selected card's details slide up under the grid. Bigger cards, so the part models read best; details only show once you pick a card.

C · Hangar

The dino fills the middle with its four sockets beside it like a mech bay, and the cards run along the bottom as a scrolling hand. The most "game" feeling; fewer cards visible at once and details pop over the hand.

What changes in the game

  • The skill tree screen goes away. The Lab buys steps (1-2-3-4 points, the same rules: 40 points, 3 masteries) and equips parts; the server checks every buy and slot as it does today.
  • Part cards show the real part: each mech part's model is built once for the card from the same code that builds it on your dino.
  • The big dino shows every upgrade applied: equipped button parts and bought always-on parts (Blade Claws). Equipping a card puts it on the dino at once.
  • Main menu: the loadout picker is replaced by a read-only slot row plus GO TO THE LAB. On the island, SKILLS (K on PC) opens the compact Lab over the game.
  • T-Rex parts that aren't built yet show as locked "COMING SOON" cards.
