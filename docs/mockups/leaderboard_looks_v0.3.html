Leaderboard looks

Real models built in Studio and standing on the islet either side of the ARENA gate (LOBBY left, WORLDWIDE right), seen from where players spawn and up close. Names on the screens are made up.

A · Fossil tablet

A carved stone slab on a rock plinth, framed in bone, with the title on a bone bar, a dino skull and an amber light on top, ribs fanning out the sides and ferns at the base. Most "dig site".

B · Mecha monitor

A heavy metal monitor with the gate's neon orange trim, dorsal plates down the sides, claw slashes, a steel skull with glowing eyes on top, on a hydraulic post bolted into rock. Matches the Rift Gate most.

C · Rib cage arch

A giant fossil rib cage rising out of a rock, a skull crowning the spine, the screen hanging inside on chains with a neon bar. Biggest silhouette; the screen is the smallest of the three.

For any of them

  • Every part is adjustable after you pick: size (they can grow to match the 90-stud gate), spacing from the gate, which pieces stay.
  • Pieces can mix: e.g. B's monitor with A's bone frame and skull, or C's rib arch around B's monitor.
  • Real data goes on the screens when built: LOBBY = players on this server, WORLDWIDE = all-time top 10, badges for the top 100.
