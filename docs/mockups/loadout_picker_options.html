Loadout: tap a slot, then tap a part

The SKILLS screen as a raptor with every part unlocked. Now a slot tap cycles through parts one by one. Both options below let you pick the slot first and then the part you want. Try it: tap a slot, then a part.

RAPTOR12 PTS
LOADOUTTap a slot to choose its part

Same in both

A part already in another slot moves to the slot you picked (the old slot empties), so a part is never in two slots.

"Empty" clears the slot. Tapping the selected slot again, or anywhere outside, cancels.

Only parts this dino owns can be picked. Passive parts (Blade Claws, stats) never show.
