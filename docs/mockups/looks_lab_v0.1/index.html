Looks Lab v0.1

Two changes to the Lab's SKINS / EMOTES / TRAILS tabs. 1: the ALL / OWNED / EARN / ROBUX row is gone. What you own comes first, then the locked ones, and each locked tile says why: a yellow BUY with the price, or the challenge with your progress. 2: the emote wheel is edited by tapping a slot on the wheel, then an emote from your list. The wheel has 8 even sections. Tap around: tabs, tiles, wheel slots and buttons all work.

The wheel's sections here are drawn as true pie slices. In the game, 8 even slices with lines between them can be built from plain UI. A filled, glowing slice for the selected slot needs one wedge image uploaded (you would upload it; I can't). Without it, the selected slot lights up its label and its two lines instead.
