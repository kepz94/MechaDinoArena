Main Menu & Dino Select

Three layouts for the first screen a player sees. Every option has SANDBOX (today's island with bots, no player damage) and ARENA (same island, bots off, player damage on), plus the dino select: a 3D preview of the current dino. Switch dinos with the arrows, tap the dino to flip it to its mech view, and tap a loadout slot to put a different mech part on it. The mech view updates to show the parts you've equipped. Switch between PC and phone (landscape, how Roblox phones play) to check spacing.

A · One-screen lobby

Dino stage on the left, title and the two mode buttons on the right. Everything in one view: pick a dino, tap a mode, you spawn. Fewest taps, and it fits a phone without scrolling.

B · Two steps

Big SANDBOX / ARENA blocks first, then a full dino select screen with PLAY and BACK. Each screen does one job. One extra tap every time you play.

C · Mode cards

Two big picture cards for the modes; your dino sits in a pill at the top that opens the dino select over the menu. The modes get the spotlight, and the dino is one tap away.

Same in every option

  • Shown when you join, and from a MENU button in the game (it replaces the "RAPTOR ▾" pill switch).
  • The dino preview is your real dino model turning slowly; the mech view adds the parts in its 4 loadout slots. The T-Rex has no live parts yet, so its mech view says so.
  • Customize: tap a slot (R / C / T / V), then a part from the row (or Empty), the same tap-a-slot-then-a-part flow as the SKILLS screen; a part already in another slot moves over. The loadout saves exactly as it does today.
  • Select other dinos with the arrows (T-Rex, Raptor; new dinos appear here automatically).
  • Sizes scale with the screen (Roblox Scale UI) with phone minimums: text at least 11-12 px, tap targets at least 40 px; nothing under the Roblox top-left buttons.
  • Fredoka One titles, Gotham text, dark panels and the yellow / orange accents already used by the HUD and SKILLS screen.
