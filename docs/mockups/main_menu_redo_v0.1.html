Main menu redo

Three layouts based on how other games lay out their lobby screens. They're clickable: change the dino with the arrows, flip MECH VIEW, and press PLAY or LAB. The backgrounds are real captures of the menu hangar from Studio. "NOW" shows the current menu for comparison.

RECOMMENDED

A · Hero center

  • Big dino in the middle, PLAY in the bottom-right thumb corner. This is the Brawl Stars / Fortnite lobby layout.
  • The dino picker sits right under the dino, with its parts next to it.
  • Each corner has one job: Roblox buttons top left, settings top right, your record bottom left, PLAY bottom right.
  • Trade-off: LAB and TUTORIAL go in a small left rail, so they're less in your face.

B · Side panel

  • Dino on the left, and everything about it in one panel on the right: name, stats, parts, LAB, record, then PLAY at the bottom.
  • Reads like a loadout screen. It's the clearest about what your dino has.
  • Trade-off: the dino gets less room, and the panel is busier on small phones.

C · Dino dock

  • All 8 dinos as cards along the bottom: one tap to switch, no arrows. PLAY sits at the end of the dock.
  • Shows the whole roster at once, which is good for "what can I get".
  • Trade-off: the dock takes the bottom of the screen, and parts and record move up top.
  • The grey cards would show each dino's picture in the real build.

What the layouts are based on: home screens keep one big character in the middle and one big bright PLAY button that's the most obvious thing on screen (Brawl Stars menu guide, Brawl Stars design critique). In landscape on phones, the key buttons go toward the corners where your thumbs are, and the layout respects the safe areas (Unity mobile UI best practices). Action games tend to use side menus rather than centered stacks (Roblox DevForum).
