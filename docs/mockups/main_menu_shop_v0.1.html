Main menu shop

Heads up: while the "everything unlocked" test switch is on, every player owns every skin, so a real shop would be empty. It's worth turning that off when the shop goes live. Three ways in and three shop screens, based on how other games do it. Tap SHOP on the menu to open it. The shop only lists Robux skins you don't own yet (this demo player owns Arctic and Lava). Tap an item to see it on your dino in 3D, use the arrows to try it on another dino, and press BUY.

RECOMMENDED

A · Featured + rows

  • Way in: an orange SHOP tile in the left rail, between LAB and TUTORIAL. Big, near the thumb, a red dot when there's something new.
  • Screen: 2 big FEATURED skins on top with a timer, then every skin you don't own in rows by price (149 / 499 / 999). Preview and BUY on the right.
  • Like Fortnite's item shop and Brawl Stars: a featured spot gives players a reason to check back.
  • New work: a featured pick that changes every day (same for everyone).

B · Store tabs

  • Way in: an orange R$ SHOP pill in the top bar, next to SETTINGS.
  • Screen: one big hero skin with BUY, tabs FEATURED / 149 / 499 / 999 / ANIMATED, a row of cards under it.
  • Like the Call of Duty store: one big item at a time, very visual.
  • On phones the top bar is a reach and the rows hold fewer items.

C · Simple list

  • Way in: a SHOP button stacked on top of PLAY (bottom right).
  • Screen: one grid of everything you don't own, cheapest first, with the preview and BUY beside it. Same layout as the Lab's SKINS tab.
  • Least new work (reuses the SKINS tab pieces), no featured spot or timer.
  • Sits next to PLAY, so it can steal taps meant for PLAY.

Based on: shops are easiest to find with one clear button that matches the rest of the menu, and new or rotating stock gives players a reason to come back (Roblox: monetization foundations); offers can also sit where players wait, like the lobby or before a round (Roblox: contextual purchases). Featured-on-top shops follow Fortnite's item shop and Brawl Stars; the hero + tabs store follows the Call of Duty store.
