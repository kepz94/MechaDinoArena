End of the match: results, MVP & XP

Today (HardpointHUD 1.2, HardpointServer 1.4): a 15 s board with VICTORY / DEFEAT / DRAW, K, D and time on the point, then the next match (Arena) or the team screen (bots match). Skill XP comes only from bot kills (half the bot's health, ×10% in the bots match). Player kills, time on the point, winning and MVP give nothing. Four picks below; the game screen plays whatever you pick.
