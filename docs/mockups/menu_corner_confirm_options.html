MENU + SKILLS to the top-right corner, and a "leave?" check

MENU and SKILLS go right up into the top-right corner of the screen instead of sitting under Roblox's top bar. Tapping MENU asks first, because it takes you off the island. Pick a confirm style below, then tap MENU inside the phone to try it.

Placement
Confirm

Tap MENU in the phone to try the confirm.

RECOMMENDED

A · Centre card

  • A card in the middle: "Leave the island?" with STAY and LEAVE.
  • Hard to hit LEAVE by accident: it sits away from where your thumb was.
  • The game keeps running behind it (your dino is not paused).

B · Tap twice

  • The first tap turns MENU red: "TAP AGAIN TO LEAVE".
  • A second tap within 3 s leaves; otherwise it goes back to MENU.
  • Fastest, but a nervous double-tap still leaves.

C · Drop-down

  • A small box opens right under MENU: "Leave the island?" LEAVE / STAY.
  • Stays in the corner, covers little of the fight.
  • LEAVE is close to where you just tapped.

Same for all

  • Order stays: SKILLS, then MENU on the far right.
  • Kept clear of the phone's notch and rounded corner.
  • PC: the M key asks the same way.
