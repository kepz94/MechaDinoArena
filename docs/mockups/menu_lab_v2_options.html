Main menu

RECOMMENDED

A · Hangar bay

A built hangar fills the whole screen behind the menu: a wall with panel lines, a hazard stripe, and a floor grid. Your dino stands on a lit pedestal on the left. The right column and the GO TO THE LAB button stay where they are today.

  • Feels like a place, not a panel over the game.
  • Layout players already know.
  • The hangar is drawn in the 3D preview, so it costs nothing in the world.

B · Title screen

A centred console-game layout: a big logo on top, your dino in the middle with ◀ ▶ beside it, and three buttons along the bottom: LAB, SANDBOX and ARENA.

  • Strongest "game title" feel.
  • The slot row moves under the dino's name.
  • Moves every button, so it is the biggest change.

C · Today's layout, solid

The same menu as today on an opaque dark background with a faint grid. The island and HUD no longer show through.

  • Smallest change.
  • Reads like a settings page, less like a game.

Lab · STATS tab

RECOMMENDED

1 · Stat rows + part cards

The four stat rows come first. Under an ALWAYS ON PARTS label sit the always-on parts as the same 3D cards used on MECHA ABILITIES. For the raptor that is Blade Claws; for the T-Rex, Bio-Battery Spine and the other always-on parts.

2 · One list

Every entry is a row: the stats with a coloured area icon, and the always-on parts with a small turning model thumbnail. Compact, but the parts lose their big card.

Both

  • MECHA ABILITIES lists only the parts you equip into a slot (R / C / T / V).
  • The bar now sits below the Roblox buttons on phones (already fixed in Studio).
