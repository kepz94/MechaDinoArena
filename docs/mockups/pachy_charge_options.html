Pachy headbutt charge

The headbutt charge is the pachy's ATTACK (left click on PC, big yellow button on phones). It comes with 2 charges, 3 when its attack line is maxed. Using the last charge slows you for a moment. Pick how the charges show; flip the states to see each one.

RECOMMENDED

A · Pips

  • One dot per charge under the ATTACK tile / button: filled = ready, filling = refilling.
  • Easy to read at a glance mid-fight, and a 3rd dot appearing makes the upgrade feel big.

B · Number badge

  • A yellow number on the corner of the tile / button.
  • Smaller, but harder to see the refill.

Same either way

  • Charging: the tile / button glows.
  • All charges used: a red "SLOWED" tag for the 2.5 s self-slow.
