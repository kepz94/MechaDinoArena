Pachy Med-Station

Health line for the pachy, in a slot. Drop a machine at your feet; while you stand in its circle you heal 50% of max health over 5 s (pachy: +45). Step out and the heal pauses; the machine still ends at 5 s. Can't drop while pinned or stunned. 30 s cooldown from the drop. Pick a machine look; press the tile to try it.
