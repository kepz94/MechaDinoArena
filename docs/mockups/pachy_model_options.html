RECOMMENDED

Knob ring + Real size

  • Matches the photo 1 look: bone dome with a ring of knobs, short snout knobs.
  • Real size is a bit bigger than the raptor (15 vs 12.9 studs), so it reads as a mid-weight between raptor and T-Rex.
  • Photo 2's orange and maroon becomes a skin (Ember).

Spikes

  • Taller spikes behind the dome and on the snout, like photo 2.
  • Reads tougher; the spikes are their own colour group, so skins can make them glow.

Bulked size

  • About 20 studs long: halfway between raptor and T-Rex, easier to hit and to see.
  • Bigger than the real animal; better if it's meant to tank hits.

Same either way

  • Same 17-joint rig as the raptor and T-Rex, so walking, turning, hit boxes and mecha parts work the same way.
  • Every colour group has its own colour on the classic look, so skins never mix groups. Pupils and mouth are never repainted.
  • Abilities (head-butt charge etc.) come after the model.
