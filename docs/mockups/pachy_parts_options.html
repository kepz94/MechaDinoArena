Pachy mecha parts: Mecha Legs & Grappling Hook

Your picks so far: Mecha Legs is a new pachy-only Speed line (I-III better leap, star = double jump with the legs shown); the Grappling Hook is an Attack button part that grabs players and bots, on its own cooldown, pulls them in and smashes for 10 with a knockback and no slow. Pick a look for each and how the hook aims. The hook sits on the right arm, slim, and the pachy raises that arm to shoot (Kepu 2026-10-30).
