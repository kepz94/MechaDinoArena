Tether aim mode

Tap Tether Spike: the camera switches to a launcher view, the pachy faces where you aim and walks / strafes without turning, and the landing circle + arc follow your aim (camera look = direction, tilt = distance, up to 90 studs). Pick the view.

CANCELFIRE

A · Over-the-shoulder artillery

Camera jumps up and behind the launcher, tilted toward the landing zone. A dashed arc shows the lob and the circle shows where it lands. You walk and strafe; the camera turns with your aim.

TETHER AIM
CANCELFIRE

B · Top-down tactical

Camera rises high above the pachy and looks almost straight down, like a map. Easiest to judge distance and see who is in the circle; you see less of what is around you.

TETHER AIM
CANCELFIRE

C · Launcher scope

Camera sits on the tail launcher looking down its barrel with a scope ring. Most "I am the launcher"; hardest to see your own pachy while strafing.

The HUD buttons (FIRE / CANCEL) are placeholders; how you fire and cancel is the next question.
