Pachy Tether Spike

Engineering button. Aim and fire a spike (like the hook and ink). If it hits a dino, the spike slams into the ground under them and a chain links them to it: they can move only inside the chain's reach until it breaks. Pick where the launcher sits, then the numbers.

RECOMMENDED

A · Back harpoon

A dark launcher on the back with a steel barrel pointing forward over the shoulders and a yellow chain drum. Clear from every angle, away from the head.

B · Tail-base launcher

Sits on top of the tail base and fires forward over the back. Sleek, out of the way of the other parts.

C · Left-arm launcher

On the left forearm, mirroring the Grappling Hook on the right arm. Reads as a matched pair of arm tools.
