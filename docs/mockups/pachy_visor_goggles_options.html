Dome Visor goggles

The plate on the head is gone. The pachy wears steel goggles with cyan lenses all the time; pressing the button makes them project a force field that blocks front hits (bite, pounce, headbutt, hook smash, Shock Cannon, ink) for 1.5 s / 2 s. A blocked hit still shows BLOCK. Pick the force field shape.

RECOMMENDED

A · Curved shield wall

The goggles glow and a curved see-through cyan wall appears just in front of the face. It covers exactly what the visor blocks (the front), so players can read it at a glance.

B · Front half-bubble

A big quarter-dome bubble covers the front half of the pachy, head to front feet. Bigger and flashier; reads as "shielded" from far away.

C · Goggle beam + disc

Two beams shoot out of the lenses and open a round energy disc in front of the snout. Most "projected by the goggles", smallest shape.

Everything else stays: steps (1.5 s · 2 s · 10 s recharge · ★ knockback), front = 60° each side, BLOCK pop, Lab card shows the goggles with the field on.
