PHONE HUD · WHAT'S ON SCREEN AND HOW TO FREE IT UP

Measured in Studio on an emulated iPhone 17 Pro (landscape, 750 × 381 screen units), Herrera in a practice match. Every box below is the real size and spot.

1 · YOUR PHONE SCREEN TODAY

  1. 4 MENU BUTTONS TOP-RIGHT — SKILLS, MENU, SQUAD, CLASSIC take a 165 × 110 block you almost never touch mid-fight.
  2. 3 STACKED INFO BOXES TOP-LEFT — score card, POINT OPEN, kill feed: 176 × 176 of the left side.
  3. A FULL-WIDTH COMPASS — 255 wide across the top, saying what the P1 marker in the world already shows.
  4. HEALTH BAR UNDER YOUR THUMB — 270 wide at the bottom-left, right where the move stick sits. Your thumb covers it.
  5. A FIXED MOVE STICK — always drawn in the corner; the big games put it wherever your thumb lands.
  6. 2 OF YOUR 4 PARTS ARE HIDDEN — slots 3 and 4 only fire with a pull-down on a slot; easy to miss.
  7. 9 GAME BUTTONS + ROBLOX'S 4 — the big games keep 5–6 controls showing at once.

2 · HOW THE BIG MOBILE GAMES DO IT

  • Fights live in the two bottom corners. Move stick left, attack cluster right, where thumbs rest (Fortnite, CoD Mobile, Brawl Stars, Roblox's own guide).
  • Info goes top-centre, small. Score, timer and objective in one strip; maps and extras collapse (Fortnite).
  • 5–6 controls on screen at most. Buttons show up when they're usable (contextual), the rest stays tucked away (Fortnite, Roblox).
  • The move stick appears where you touch (dynamic stick) instead of a fixed circle (Fortnite).
  • One fat main button with skills in an arc around it — CoD Mobile's fire button with grenades / operator skill / scorestreaks around it.
  • Players can move, resize and fade every button — CoD Mobile's Custom Layout (size + opacity per button).
  • Buttons at least 44 points and text 18 pt or bigger so nothing gets mis-tapped (Fortnite).
