PLAYTIME REWARDS · v0.1

Rewards for staying longer in one session: skill points along the way, an exclusive skin at the end. The timer lives on the server and keeps running through Arena matches and back (it resets when you leave the game). The HUD below is a real island capture; the reward UI on top is the mockup. Tap the chip and the CLAIM buttons.
