Now · why it looks buggy

Live 1.6 rules

  • It can never go above 208 px from the top. A phone screen is 390 px tall, so the top half is off limits and the marker slides along that line instead of sitting on the point.
  • Near the score card it jumps down under the pills and kill feed, about 100 px in one frame.
  • It hangs 34 studs over the point and is drawn above that, so up close it floats high in the sky.
  • It pops out the instant that spot leaves the screen, and stays full size on your crosshair and while you stand on the point.
A · CoD (recommended)

Rides the screen edge

  • Pinned just above the point and centred on it, so it sits exactly where the point is. No height limit, no jumps.
  • When the point leaves the screen, the marker slides to the screen edge on that side and stays there, gliding (CoD does this).
  • Goes see-through near the middle of the screen so it never blocks your aim.
  • Hides while you're on the point (the pill says SECURED).
  • Same size at any distance; distance under it.
B · Calmer

Fades out off screen

  • Everything in A, but when the point leaves the screen the marker fades away instead of riding the edge.
  • The compass is your only arrow when the point is behind you.
  • Less on screen, but you lose the edge hint CoD gives.
