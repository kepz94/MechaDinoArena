1What sits behind the results

What happens now (from the code, not checked live): after the last title card the camera goes back to your dino, and the results board (760 × 400) sits on top of the live game with no dim. The match ending also lets go of the respawn wait, so a dino that died at the end gets placed on a spawn while the board is up. Players can still run around behind it.

A · Stay on the stage

After the last title the camera stays up on the sky stage where the titles played. The last title's dino keeps idling on the pad, the camera turns slowly, light dim behind the board. The live game is never shown again before the trip.

B · Solid backdrop

A dark blue full-screen backdrop behind the board (red on a loss). Nothing of the game shows. Simplest and cheapest on phones, but it feels flatter than A.

C · Blur the game

The live game stays behind a strong blur and a 60% dim. Cheap, but you can still make out movement, so it only half fixes "the game is still running".

In every option

  • No respawning during the results: a dino that died at the end stays parked until the trip.
  • The respawn screen and HUD stay hidden until the trip.
  • Draws and matches with no titles get the same backdrop from the banner on.
