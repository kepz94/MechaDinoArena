What other games do

Practice vs AI

  • Call of Duty: Mobile: you pick a mode, and bots fill a 5v5 match. XP is cut 90%, so it's for practice. Players complain that the bots move in a scripted, repetitive way and stand in the open.
  • Overwatch: 5v5. The bot team always has a sensible mix (1 tank, 2 damage, 2 support). Three difficulties change damage, accuracy, how often bots use abilities and how they move. Bots group up behind the tank on the way to the objective, retreat when outnumbered, go after healers first and swap heroes after dying a lot.

Making bots feel like players

  • Only do what a player could do: no instant reactions, no perfect aim, and no knowing where someone is behind a wall (Unvanquished's bot rules).
  • Skill levels stack: an easy bot knows a few tricks, and a hard bot knows all of them.
  • Random personalities (pushy, careful, sneaky) make them less predictable.
  • Kill feed: killer, then how they killed, then the victim. Lines with you in them are highlighted and stay longer (Team Fortress 2). Call of Duty lets you turn it off or set how long it stays.
  • Teams: games auto-balance by moving someone from the bigger team, but not in the last minute (Team Fortress 2).

Reacts in 0.8 s, sees 70 studs, uses a ready ability about 1 time in 4, never retreats. For new players.

Hard

Reacts in 0.3 s, sees 110 studs, uses abilities when the tag fits, rotates early and focuses the weakest enemy.

Two questions before the plan

RECOMMENDED

1 · Bots use the real abilities

Every ability server gets a second way in, so a bot fires exactly the same code as a player. Damage, cooldowns, effects and every later balance change are the same for bots automatically. It's done one dino at a time (Raptor first) because there are about 51 parts plus 8 base kits.

2 · Bots get their own copies

The bot script gets a simple version of each ability. It's faster to start, but every later change has to be made twice, and the copies drift away from the real ones.

  • Pick A, B or C for the team pick and the kill feed spot.
  • Abilities: 1 or 2? With 1, the first build is the match, the teams, the kill feed and bots that play the point with their basic attacks. Then the abilities go in one dino at a time.
  • The Sandbox's roaming bot packs, prey and territories switch off while the practice match is on (one switch turns them back on). The Arena stays players only.
