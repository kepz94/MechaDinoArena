MECHA DINO ARENA · MOCKUP v0.3 · PRE-GAME (B · LINEUP) · NEW SELECT SCREEN

Lineup → Select → Intro

v0.3: the SELECT screen is new (lineup and intros as v0.2). You load into the lineup. Tap your pedestal (or SELECT DINO) to open the select screen, pick a dino and its loadout, CONFIRM, and you're back on the lineup with your pick standing on your pedestal. Teams are 4 v 4 (Arena and bots). 30 seconds for everyone; when it runs out (or everyone is ready) the player intros play. Teammates pick on their own in this mockup.

HOW OTHER GAMES DO IT (WHAT WE TOOK)

  • Rainbow Six Siege: pick from the roster, then adjust that operator's loadout; an operator is one per team per round. → separate SELECT screen with the dino and its 4 slots; picked dinos show who took them.
  • VALORANT: the faceoff screen lets player banners fill the screen (card, title, rank) and they cut decoration for one clear call to action. → the lineup has one button (SELECT DINO) and the plaques carry the W-L.
  • Apex Legends: the match-start squad banner shows each player's stat trackers. → W-L plaques under each pedestal.
  • NBA 2K: starting-lineup intros, one player at a time with their numbers. → spotlight intros.
