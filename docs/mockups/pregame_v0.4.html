Lineup → Select → Parts → Intro

The dinos and mecha parts here are the game's own models, exported from Studio block by block. Tap your pedestal (or SELECT DINO), pick a dino and press SELECT: the same screen slides into its mecha parts. Point at a part to see it on the dino, tap a slot then a part to equip it. Drag the dino to spin it. 4 v 4, 30 seconds for everyone.

WHAT'S NEW IN v0.4

  • Every dino is the real model (exported from Studio: DinoModelBuilder + each dino's ModelData), on the lineup pedestals, in the select screen and in the intros.
  • SELECT slides the same screen into the mecha parts: the roster moves away, the dino takes the space, the parts list opens.
  • Each part is the Lab's own 3D preview built on that dino; pointing at it shows it on the model and turns the camera to it. Equipped parts stay on.
