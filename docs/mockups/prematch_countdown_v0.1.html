WHAT COD DOES

  • After the infil, a countdown runs before the match: everyone is held in place (you can look around, not move or fire).
  • Mode name and objective on screen; the first objective's marker is already in the world, and it shows on the minimap.
  • Ticks on the last seconds, then the match starts. Hardpoint's first point goes live a few seconds in.

Closest to CoD. Big number in the middle, HARDPOINT, the objective, how you score, and a FIRST POINT pill. The P1 marker pulses in the world with its distance.

B · MAP CARD

Countdown at the top. A map card shows the island, all 5 points, P1 lit, you, and a dotted line from you to P1. Teaches the rotation too.

C · POINT FLYOVER

Seconds 10–6 the camera shows P1 itself ("FIRST POINT · CENTRE MEADOW"), then back to your dino for 5–1 with the objective. Most cinematic; you see the real place.
