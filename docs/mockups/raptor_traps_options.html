Raptor Traps

An ambush part for the roamer. Set a trap where a dino will walk, get a ping when it springs, then come in with Pounce, Blade Claws or the Shock Cannon. Every option below alerts you when it springs and doesn't copy the cannon's stun. View: the default game camera. A T-Rex walks across and steps on the trap.

A · Snare

Dropped at your feet and hard to see (faint outline). Steel jaws snap shut and chain the dino to the spot: it can't walk away but can still turn, bite and use parts. This holds a target in place, which the cannon's stun doesn't. Good for covering an escape or baiting a chase.

Place
At your feet
Effect
Rooted 1.5 s (2 s at ★)
Seen by enemy
Faint outline from close up

B · Shock mine

Lobbed up to 30 studs ahead with a blinking light. Bursts with a jolt: some damage and a slow. Good for zone control in a fight, but it's closest to what the cannon already does (electric hit).

Place
Thrown 30 studs
Effect
15 damage + 50% slow 2 s
Seen by enemy
Blinking light, easy to spot

C · Tripwire net

Two posts with a red laser between them, set across a path. Crossing it drops a net: a big slow, and it shows the dino through walls to you for a while. Best for watching routes, and great with Scent, but it takes two taps to set.

Place
Post here, then post there
Effect
60% slow 3 s + revealed 5 s
Seen by enemy
Visible laser
