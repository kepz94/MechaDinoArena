Step texts on the new parts are first drafts for you to edit; numbers follow the 35% rule and get tuned when each part is built. Thrusters is live; Energy Shield and Grapple Hook keep their existing step texts.

What this means for the T-Rex

The tank parts you set aside (Med-Station, Back Turret, Shoulder Rocket, Armor Plating, Sentry, Traps, Barricade) are the T-Rex's candidates, plus a T-Rex speed part to replace Thrusters. We plan those next.

Today's loadout has 2 slots, and the raptor would have 8 button parts: Adrenaline, Dart, Cloak, Shield, Thrusters, Grapple, Beacon and Decoy. Choosing which 2 to bring is the build choice. Blade Claws is passive and needs no slot.
