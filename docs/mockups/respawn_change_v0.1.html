SWAP DINO + LOADOUT WHEN YOU RESPAWN

Today you're back in 3 s as the same dino; changing means MENU and leaving the match. Here: a 5 s respawn wait where you can pick another dino and its 4 mecha parts, and a CHANGE button that stays a few seconds after you're back in. The dinos are the game's own models. The loop plays: dead (5 s) → you spawn → the CHANGE button for 5 s.

1 · PICK THE RESPAWN SCREEN

2 · IF YOU'RE STILL PICKING WHEN THE TIMER HITS 0

1 · WAIT FOR MERECOMMENDED

The respawn holds while the picker is open, up to 10 s more, then you spawn with what you've picked. Like CoD's class select.

2 · SPAWN ANYWAY

You spawn at 0 with your current pick; the picker stays open over the game during the after-spawn seconds.

3 · AFTER YOU SPAWN, THE CHANGE BUTTON STAYS…

1 · 5 S, OR UNTIL YOU ATTACK / GET HITRECOMMENDED

A swap puts you back on your spawn spot as the new dino at full health. Ending it on your first attack or hit stops "swap mid-fight to heal".

2 · 5 S NO MATTER WHAT

Exactly 5 s, even in a fight.

3 · 3 S

Shorter window, same rules as 1.

ALL OPTIONS
  • Phone and PC: tap the tiles / button, or on PC the same plus the key C for CHANGE.
  • The picked dino and parts are what you spawn as from then on (and saved like today's last dino).
  • Only in a match (Arena and practice). The island keeps today's 3 s respawn.
  • Bots are unchanged.
