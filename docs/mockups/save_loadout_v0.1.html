Save a loadout in the Lab, get it in the pre-game

OPTION STATE
LAB (RAPTOR)
PRE-GAME · SELECT RAPTOR → PARTS STEP

A · SAVE IN THE SLOT ROW

A fifth tile at the end of the 4 slots: yellow SAVE LOADOUT while the slots differ from the saved ones, green SAVED ✓ when they match.

B · SAVED CHIP

A small line over the slots: "LOADOUT · SAVED ✓" or "UNSAVED" + a SAVE button. The slot row keeps its full width.

PRE-GAME (BOTH)

Picking a dino puts its saved loadout in your 4 slots and shows SAVED LOADOUT. You can still change slots there; that doesn't change what's saved. No saved loadout yet → your current slots, as today.
