Scent Sense mask

Scent Sense gets a part you can see: a mask over the raptor's nose and mouth. It shows while Scent Sense is bought and in a slot, like the other parts, and it becomes the 3D model on its Lab card. The upper half sits on the snout and the lower half on the jaw, so the jaw still opens to bite. Tap "Bite" to check that.

Same for every option

  • Built by ScentServer on the raptor's Head and Jaw. The Lab card uses the same build code, through a BuildPreview hook like the other parts.
  • The volt parts glow brighter while a sniff is running.
  • It ships maxed and unlocked through the test switch, like the other parts.
