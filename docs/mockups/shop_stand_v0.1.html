Island shop stand

From the bridge end of the islet: LOBBY board, ARENA gate, WORLDWIDE board. Every player spawns on the arc in front of the gate, so a stand at either outer side of a board is in view the moment you land. Pick a spot and a look. The yellow ring is where walking up shows the shop button.

1 · WALK UP

Inside the ring (about 14 studs) a big yellow button shows over your attack area. Phone and PC. Leave the ring and it goes.

OPEN SHOP

2 · SHOP OPENS

The same Shop as the menu tile: SKINS, EMOTES, TRAILS, FEATURED, BUY. Your dino stands still while it is open.

3 · CLOSE

The × closes it and you are back on the island where you stood. Nothing else on the island changes.

Same for every option: the boards, gate, spawns and bridge stay where they are. The stand is built by a new server script on the island only (not in matches), with its own switch; every size and spot is an attribute.
