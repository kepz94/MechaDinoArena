Classic skill tree: three layouts

These are connected nodes, like a classic RPG talent tree. Every path starts from your dino, splits into its 5 areas, then into lines. Each line has 4 steps: I, II, III and ★. Round nodes are dino stats; hexagons are mecha parts. A gold ring and glow marks your mastered lines. Only area names are written on the tree; click a node to see its name and steps on the right, then press UPGRADE.
