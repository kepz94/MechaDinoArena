Buying skins with Robux

Each paid skin is a game pass. Tap a locked skin, then GET · R$. Roblox opens its own purchase window (we can't change how that looks). Pick what happens after a player buys. In the stand-in window you can press Buy or Cancel.

After buying
RECOMMENDED

A · Buy & wear

  • After Buy, the skin goes on your dino straight away and saves, same as EQUIP.
  • Toast "LAVA UNLOCKED!" in gold, the card flashes, badge turns ON.
  • One tap from wanting it to wearing it. Small build.

B · Unlock moment

  • A full-screen reveal: rarity glow, the dino bobbing in the new skin, big name.
  • WEAR IT puts it on; LATER keeps your current skin.
  • Feels the most like a game; the biggest build of the three.

C · Unlock only

  • The card unlocks and the button changes to EQUIP. You tap EQUIP yourself.
  • Toast "LAVA UNLOCKED". Smallest build, one extra tap.

Same in all three

  • Cancel in the Roblox window: nothing changes, no message.
  • Ownership comes from Roblox (the pass), so a bought skin can't be lost by our save, and it shows on any server and device.
  • Passes also appear in the game page's Store tab, so players can buy there too.
  • Prices on the cards are placeholders; the real price is whatever you set on each pass.
