Skins as a third Lab tab

The Lab gets MECHA ABILITIES · STATS · SKINS. Tap a skin card and the dino on the Lab stage previews it; EQUIP puts it on your real dino and saves it, so you spawn in it every time until you pick another. Locked skins show their Robux price; buying comes in the next step. Use the chips to see the Lab from the menu and on the island.

Skins only in the menu Lab

  • Matches "pick it in the menu": the SKINS tab shows in the full Lab (main menu → GO TO THE LAB).
  • On the island the compact Lab keeps its two tabs, so nobody changes colour mid-fight.

Skins on the island too

  • The compact Lab on the island also gets the SKINS tab, and EQUIP recolours your dino right there.
  • Handy, but a dino could change colour in the middle of a fight.

Same either way

  • Cards: a small model in that skin, name, rarity; EQUIPPED / price badge.
  • Right panel: the skin's colours, then EQUIP / EQUIPPED / GET · R$ (greyed until buying is built).
  • Each dino has its own skins; colours only, never hitboxes or stats.
