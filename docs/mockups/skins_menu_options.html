Pick a skin in the main menu

Your dino in the hangar recolours the moment you tap a skin, and that's the skin you spawn with every time until you pick another. Tap skins inside the phone to try each option. Locked skins show where a Robux price would go; buying isn't part of the first build unless you want it to be.

Option

A · Swatch strip

  • A row of colour swatches under the dino's name; one tap = equipped.
  • Fastest to use, takes no extra screen.
  • Tight on a phone once there are more than ~8 skins; no room for names or rarity.
RECOMMENDED

B · Skins locker

  • A SKINS button beside GO TO THE LAB opens a locker over the right side: cards with name, rarity and owned / price.
  • Tap a card to preview it on your dino; EQUIP keeps it (locked ones show the price instead).
  • Room to grow: an ACCESSORIES tab can be added later without moving anything.

C · Arrows on the dino

  • ◀ skin name ▶ right under the dino, like the dino switcher.
  • Simplest build, nothing new on screen.
  • Players can't see all skins at once, so selling them is harder.

Same for all

  • The skin you equip is saved and goes on every new body: respawns and dino switches.
  • Each dino has its own skins (a raptor skin stays a raptor skin).
  • Skins only change colours, never hitboxes or stats.
