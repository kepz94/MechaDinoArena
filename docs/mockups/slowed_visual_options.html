Pachy headbutt: slowed look on the dino

The same look shows on the dino that got headbutted (slowed 3 s) and on the pachy after it uses its last charge (slowed 2.5 s). Everyone near it sees it. Pick a look; the scene plays the slow from start to end.

  • The dino glows a cold purple, drags heavy dust at its feet and leaves fading after-images, so it reads as sluggish from any distance.
  • Very visible; no text, so a new player may not know it means slowed.

  • Fades out together when the slow ends.
