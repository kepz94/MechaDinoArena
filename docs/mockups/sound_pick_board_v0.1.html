Sound Pick Board

The arcade layer for Mecha Dino Arena's core sounds. Each event has three original 8-bit options made for this game (you own them, so there are no license worries). Tap a letter to hear it, choose one with "pick", then copy your picks and paste them back to Claude.

The heavier sounds (getting hit, dying, real win and lose music stings) come from Roblox's licensed sound library, so you audition those on the board in Studio Play instead.
