How it would work under the hood

One "main attack" hookLifesteal, bleed and stun steps listen to every dino's real hit (bite, pounce, headbutt and slash), and "recharges faster" points at the dino's own charge / cooldown (slash refill for the Herrera). That is why Lifesteal and Jaws II-★ do nothing for her today.
Per-dino rowsEach dino lists its rows (name, area, 4 step texts, the effect each step turns on). The Lab reads that list, so a new dino only needs its list filled in.
Saved progressSteps already bought keep their place: a row that is only renamed keeps its pips (Jaws -> Razor Claws on the Herrera). A row that is removed refunds its points.
