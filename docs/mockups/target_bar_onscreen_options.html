Keep the bot's bar on screen

The big bar at the top goes away. The bot keeps its own small floating bar (name + health), and it never leaves your screen. Use "Bot downed below you" to see the case from your screenshot, where the bar used to drift off screen.

A · Over your raptor while fighting

  • While you're hitting or pinning a dino, its small bar floats just above your raptor's head, so it's always in front of you.
  • About 4 seconds after your last hit it goes back over the bot's head.
  • Only the dino you're fighting moves; other bots keep their bars over their heads.

  • The bar stays over the bot's head, but if it would leave the screen it stops at the nearest edge with a red frame and an arrow pointing at the bot.
  • Only for the dino you're fighting.
  • It can end up at the bottom by your thumb when the bot is below you.
