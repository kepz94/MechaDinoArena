Friend or foe: who's on my team?

What Kepu saw in the live match (2026-11-10 21:42): every bot has the same white name and green health bar, the bars don't shrink with distance, and nothing says blue team or red team. Pick a direction below; flip PC / phone and the four screens.

Call of Duty

Teammates get blue names over their heads, enemies red. When Modern Warfare II's beta dropped enemy name plates, players started shooting their own team, and asked for the red plates back (GameSpot). Lesson: the colour is what tells friend from foe, not the name.

Overwatch

Teammates are blue and can be shown through walls; that view is an option, off by default, and players argue about how much team info to show (PCGamesN). Enemy bars are red. Lesson: blue / red everywhere, and keep the always-on part small.

Brawl Stars · Wild Rift · Pokémon Unite

The phone team games colour the health bar itself: yours green, teammates blue, enemies red, with short names. Every fighter keeps a bar, so it works best when the bars are small. (Not from a source I could open today; this is how those games look in play.)
