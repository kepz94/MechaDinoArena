What each switch means

Club TailThe Teno's version of the shared attack row (Jaws on the T-Rex, Razor Claws on the Herrera). No attack row = the Teno has 3 rows like the pachy, and its damage only grows from Attack points.
Off only while stunnedBuck Off does nothing while you are stunned. Once the stun ends, a raptor still latched on gets the Buck Off effects again (latch damage ticks, free slam).
Off for the whole latchIf a raptor latched on while you were stunned, Buck Off stays off for that latch until it lets go or is thrown off, even after your stun ends.
