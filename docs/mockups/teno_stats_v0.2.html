How the new rows work (proposal, numbers to confirm)

SurvivorKill heals are off for every dino today (they heal after 8 s without damage instead); this row turns them back on for the Teno only. III counts any enemy that dies within 25 studs of you, whoever killed it (no tracking, just distance). ★ refills one slam charge per kill.
Heavy TailMakes the rear tail slam itself better: +3 damage, faster refill, longer reach (the aim zone grows with it), and a slow after the stun at ★. Works with the slam rework (rear only, soft aim).
Front ClawsThe scratch is the Teno's only front attack once the slam is rear-only. +3 damage as a direct bonus to the scratch (not Attack points, so the slam doesn't grow too), a wider swipe, faster scratches, and a short slow at ★.
Armored HideThe Thick Hide / Wiry Frame line in Teno words, but step I is 10% (not 30%) because the Teno already has 150 HP. II–★ are the same effects the other dinos get: half knockback, shorter stuns, no stuns above half health.
