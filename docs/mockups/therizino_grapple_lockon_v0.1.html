Grapple Zip · lock-on

Nothing shows until you press GRAPPLE. Hold it and the grapple locks onto the furthest tree, rock or wall in front of you that it can reach; turn to switch targets; let go to zip. A quick tap zips to that same pick straight away. Try it on the map below, then pick how the lock should look.

1 · How it picks (top-down map)

hold, turn, let go
or just tap

The rules

  • Targets: trees, rocks, mountain steps and walls. Not the ground, not dinos.
  • Only ones inside the range (90 / 110 / 130 by tier) and inside a 30° cone either side of where the camera looks.
  • Of those, the furthest one you have a clear line to wins.
  • You zip to the side of it facing you, then fly on with 60% of the speed.

When nothing is in the cone

  • As now: the claw latches in the air at full range and still pulls you.
  • The lock shows a faint "air" mark at full range while you hold.

If you'd rather it do nothing here, say "no air latch".
