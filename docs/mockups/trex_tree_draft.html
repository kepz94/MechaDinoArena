T-Rex skill tree (draft)

This is your T-Rex mecha pick. The greyed cards are the shared stat paths that every dino has. The bright cards belong to the T-Rex only. Every mecha part is visible hardware on the dino; the orange line under each name says what you see. Step texts are first drafts for you to edit; numbers follow the 35% rule and get tuned when each part is built.

newbutton

Small drone docked on the back; lifts off and hovers over you, welding sparks.

newpassive

  2. Heal 20%
  3. 4 cells to fill
  4. A full battery also gives 3 s of 35% faster bite recharge
