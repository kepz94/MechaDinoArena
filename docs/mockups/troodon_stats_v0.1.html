Quick Feet II–★The list said "hop". The Troodon has no Evade Hop any more: its JUMP is Flap & Glide (2 flaps, then an auto glide). So II–★ use the flaps instead: flaps carry you 35% farther forward, then a 3rd flap, then a glide landing leaves a speed trail.
Night Watcher III / ★The list said "shows on the minimap"; the game has no minimap. III is now: squadmates see the outlines you see. ★ "unaware" is defined as: an enemy that hasn't hit anyone in the last 5 s.
