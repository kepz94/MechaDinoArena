Sandbox tutorial: pick a look

The steps follow your picks: pounce, leap, a mecha part, then skills / Lab and the menu. It plays as the raptor on a player's first Sandbox visit, can be skipped, and comes back from a TUTORIAL button in the main menu. Switch the look, then click "Done (simulate)" to walk through the steps.

RECOMMENDED

A · Coach card + pointer

A card at the top centre (where the big enemy bar was) with the step, a one-line tip and SKIP. A bouncing arrow and a pulsing ring sit on the button or the bot to use. A green tick shows when you've done it, then the next step.

B · Checklist on the left

All five steps in a list on the left, current one bright, done ones ticked green. The button to use pulses. You see the whole tutorial at once, but it covers more of the island.

C · Spotlight + robot coach

The screen dims except the button to use, with a speech bubble from a small mecha coach. Hardest to miss; the island is dark while a step is up, so it feels less like playing.

The steps (all looks)

  1. POUNCE a Raptor Bot (done when you latch on).
  2. LEAP (done when you jump).
  3. Use a mecha part button, e.g. ADRENALINE.
  4. Tap SKILLS: spend points in the Lab.
  5. MENU: switch dinos or go to the Arena (done with "Got it").
