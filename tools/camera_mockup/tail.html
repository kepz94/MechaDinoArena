The three cameras, defined for this game

the regular Roblox camera

Classic

  • Roblox's own camera (Custom, touch mode Classic): it follows the dino and never turns by itself.
  • The dino faces where it walks (TurnLimit "move": turns at TurnLimitRate, swings wide).
  • Drag the right side to orbit freely, pinch to zoom. No crosshair.
  • Built from: ShoulderCamEnabled = false (already in ShoulderCam 1.8).
what players call shift lock

Shift Lock

  • Camera centred behind the dino, raised so the dino sits under the crosshair.
  • The dino always turns to face the camera direction (TurnLimit "aim"); the stick strafes and backs up.
  • Drag turns camera and dino together. Crosshair dot on.
  • Built from: the shoulder cam with ShoulderCamSide = 0 and a higher ShoulderCamHeight. Every aim path stays as it is today.
today's camera

Shoulder

  • ShoulderCam 1.8 as shipped: camera over the right shoulder, dino on the left of the screen.
  • Same aim model as Shift Lock (body turns to the camera, strafe, crosshair).
  • PC keeps Q to swap shoulders; phones have no swap button (ArenaTouch 1.18 header, Kepu's earlier call).

Default for new players: Shift Lock on phones and tablets (it keeps every camera-aimed part working and takes away the off-centre view Kepu disliked); Shoulder stays the default on PC. The pick is saved per player in the MDA_Settings_v1 DataStore that already remembers MUSIC (MusicServer 1.0), and comes back on every join and respawn.

What changes for aiming in each camera (from the code)

Shift Lock and Shoulder share one aim model, so only Classic needs work. "Body" = the server or client uses the HumanoidRootPart's flat LookVector; "camera" = the client sends workspace.CurrentCamera.CFrame.LookVector or casts a ray from the screen centre.

Research

  • Roblox touch camera modes: Classic "tracks the player but will not automatically rotate", Follow "automatically rotates if the player walks left or right", Orbital fixed height. DevTouchCameraMovementMode, TouchCameraMovementMode
  • Roblox's player Settings offer Classic / Follow; Shift Lock Switch is a Shift-key toggle. Roblox Help: camera controls
  • Mobile has no built-in shift lock; games ship their own button UI or fork the camera module; the thumbstick feeding the mouse position is the classic pitfall (this game hit it: ShoulderCam 1.2). DevForum: Shift lock for mobile players, DevForum: Mouse lock on mobile devices, EzShiftlock (CameraModule makes no mouse-lock controller on touch)
  • How games show it: Blox Fruits, a padlock "in the lower right" that cycles three modes; Project Slayers, a lock icon under the dash button. fruityblox.com, ofzenandcomputing.com
