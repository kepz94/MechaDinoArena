Dilo kit · v0.1

Role (Kepu): ranged spitter, raptor size. The real Dilo model (DiloModelData 1.0, look E · Bruiser Face, 160 blocks) doing each proposed move, game-style: wind-up, snap, follow-through, hit-stop and venom hits on a raptor. The red and yellow hood frill stays folded at rest and snaps open when it attacks, then folds back: that is its signature look. Ranged moves fire straight ahead with a soft aim (the faint 30° cone: the closest enemy inside it gets the shot, no aim mode on phones). Jumps land plainly, no dust or shockwave. Pick one attack (A, B or C) and one jump (J1 or J2).

Gives the Dilo a clear ranged identity (it spits from far away; nothing else in the roster does), it is the simplest on a phone (tap ATTACK and it aims itself, tap JUMP to get away) and there is no aim mode. The frill opening before every glob is also a fair warning for whoever is about to get hit.

House rules this follows

  • No aim mode on phones (Kepu 2026-10-31, Herrera Goo): ranged moves fire straight ahead; the closest enemy within ~30° of facing gets the shot (the faint cone and line in the preview). A raptor outside the cone is ignored.
  • No extra dust or seismic effects on a jump landing (Kepu 2026-10-31): both jumps land plainly; J2's splash is the glob hitting the target, not the landing.
  • One phone layout for every dino: the big yellow ATTACK + the JUMP button (strip under the preview). A shows charge pips under ATTACK; B shows its spit meter as a ring; C shows the hold ring.
  • The frill: the model's 8 folded "_fold" blocks show at rest; the 48 open "_frill" blocks snap open on an attack and fold back after. In game: swap which set is visible, with an optional quick grow (as in the preview).

All numbers are proposals (tunable attributes). Damage is against a raptor (60 HP). Times in the preview are the real ones; turn on Slow motion to see the frill snap.
