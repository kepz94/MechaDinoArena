What comes from Call of Duty

  • Animated border around the zone (Modern Warfare III guide: “animated border around the objective”; Black Ops 6: the zone is marked by borders that also show on the mini-map).
  • Marker over the point with your distance (Modern Warfare 2019 Hardpoint: a target marker over the hardpoint shows your distance; it reads “contested” when an enemy is on it).
  • Lock icon on the next point about 10 s before it opens (Modern Warfare III guide).
  • Your team blue, the enemy red on your own screen; name tags in the same colours.

Ours on top: the border is built from the game’s studded blocks and neon, sized for dinos (radius 45, 90 studs across), and the colours change with the state.
