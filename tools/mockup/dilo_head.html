Dilo model · v0.1

The ranged spitter. Twin head crests like the real Dilophosaurus, plus a fold-out frill (our own design, not a copy of the film one), and a throat sac on every style for the venom. Three different styles, each its own body, posture, crests and frill. The colours are a first pass and can move between styles. Raptor size: 13 studs at scale 1, ~26 in game.

  • "Frill open" switches between the frill spread open and folded back along the neck. "Preview spit" shows a rough spit with the frill flaring open: rear back, then lunge with the jaw open. The real spit (range, damage, effect) comes in the kit plan.
