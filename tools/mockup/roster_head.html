Dino roster · v1.0

Every dino on the roster, built from its real model data in the repo, at its in-game size. Go through them one by one, or switch to the lineup to see them all side by side, smallest to biggest.

  • Sizes are in-game: the T-Rex at scale 1, every other dino doubled (as the game builds them). Lengths are nose to tail tip.
  • Live = playable in the game now. Saved = model saved in the repo, not installed in Studio yet, no moves yet (HP and speed come with each kit plan).
  • Use the arrow keys or Prev / Next to step through. Drag the view to turn it.
