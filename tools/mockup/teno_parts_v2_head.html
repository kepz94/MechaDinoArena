One kit, seven parts

The rules every look follows, so the Teno's parts read as one set and not as random boxes:

  • Armour, not boxes: housings are studded plates in the dino's own block style that wrap the arm, back or tail they sit on, with straps and bolts holding them on.
  • Machinery you can see: drums, pistons, gears and hinges that actually move when the part is used.
  • Plugged in: black hoses run from each part into the body.
  • One glow per part, in its Lab colour, at the spot the move comes out of (the hook's muzzle, the feather vents …).
  • Bone-white blades match the Teno's own hooves and beak; brass on every moving joint; yellow-black stripes only where something launches.
  • Looks only — nothing in the game changes from this page. Once you've picked, I'll plan the build (same numbers and moves as now; only the models change, plus small pose tweaks if a look needs them).
  • Sizes are on the real model at the game's scale; studs are drawn the way the game draws them.
