The 8 titles and what earns them

Who gets what

Only the winning team gets titles (up to 4 players); everyone in the server watches the show. Each player gets one title. The game goes down the list below in order and gives each title to the best teammate who earns it and doesn't have a title yet. Anyone left over gets the title they came closest to. Two players can share a title only if there aren't enough to go round.
