T-Rex redesign · v0.2

v0.2: A2 is Apex made true to life (real-size skull, head carried level with the back, longer tail), and X is A2 at roster scale (62 studs, the biggest dino like the real animal).
Three T-Rex designs with real proportions instead of the cartoon ones: a long, deep skull that is widest at the back, a small eye under a brow boss, a short thick neck, a deep ribcage, tiny arms, long powerful legs, and a long tail held level as a counterweight. They differ in build, not colour: the colours are the T-Rex's current skin colours, so its skins keep working. Same length as now (38 studs); N is the T-Rex in the game, for comparison.

  • Mockup only: the T-Rex in the game doesn't change until you pick. A redesign keeps the rig and part names the game uses, so its moves, skins and hit boxes carry over; mecha parts and the leg animation get checked in Studio as part of the install.
  • "Preview bite" is only a preview of how the head moves; the bite itself doesn't change.
