Troodon kit · v0.2

Role (Kepu 2026-11-06): area control + support. "A projectile that damages enemies and heals teammates" · shape "a shot that splashes a zone" · solo: "a small self-heal on hit". The real Troodon model (TroodonModelData 1.0, look B · Night Watcher, 188 blocks) at the 2x game body, doing each proposed move game-style: wind-up, throw, follow-through, hit-stop. Every attack ends in the same glow zone: enemies inside take damage and are slowed, squadmates inside heal (blue ring = squadmate; squads exist in the Arena), and the troodon heals a little for each enemy the splash hits, so it still matters when you play alone. Its big amber eyes flare on every throw: that is its signature look. Soft aim like the Dilo (the faint 30° cone; no aim mode on phones). Jumps land plainly. v0.2: the new jump J3 · Flap & Glide (attack A is live).

v0.2: J3 · Flap & Glide (Kepu 2026-11-06 17:38)

"Have it be able to flap up and basically start to fly but more like a triple jump and glide since it has feathers" · "Glides by itself after the 3rd flap". J3 replaces the live Evade Hop (J2, shown for comparison). It reuses two moves already in the game: the pachy's Mecha Legs air jump (here two of them) and the Herrera's Glider Wings glide (same fall rate), so it plays like moves players already know. Also live in Studio since v0.1: run 86 / walk 46 and a pod range of 80 studs (not published yet).

Proposed numbers (all tunable attributes)

  • Health 55 (Herrera 40, Raptor 60, Dilo 70): a support that has to be protected, between its size neighbours.
  • Run 74 / walk 39 (Dilo 66, Raptor 78): quick enough to reposition, slower than the hunters.
  • Glow zone: 20 studs wide (the Goo puddle is 16, the Toxin Mortar cloud 16 / 20 upgraded: this is the troodon's main move, so the upgraded size), 3 s. Enemies: 6 on the splat + 3/s, slowed 25%. Squadmates: +5/s. Troodon: +2 per enemy the splat hits. Zones don't stack on one dino (the newest one counts).
  • Against a raptor (60 HP) one pod = 15 damage; a squadmate standing in it gets +15.

House rules this follows

  • No aim mode on phones (Kepu 2026-10-31): the shot goes to the closest enemy within ~30° of facing (the cone and line), else straight ahead.
  • No dust or seismic effects on a jump landing (Kepu 2026-10-31): both jumps land plainly.
  • One phone layout for every dino: big yellow ATTACK + JUMP (strip under the preview). A shows 2 charge pips; B a cooldown ring; C the hold ring.
  • Squads: heals only reach squadmates (SquadRules, the same check the Med-Station uses); squads exist in the Arena. With no squad the self-heal is the heal side.
  • Every move: wind-up → action → result → recovery, and moves read like the older dinos' (the soft aim and the pips are the Dilo's).

Times in the preview are the real proposed ones; turn on Slow motion to see the throw. The squadmate and the enemies are raptors only because that model is in the page.
